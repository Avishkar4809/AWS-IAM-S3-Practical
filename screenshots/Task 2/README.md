Task 2: Attach IAM policies

1. Attach the AWS managed policy AmazonS3ReadOnlyAccess to the Developers group.
2. Attach the AWS managed policy AmazonS3FullAccess to the Testers group.
3. Write one or two sentences explaining the difference between these policies.

Evidence to submit: Screenshots of the policy attachments and your explanation.

Conclusion: The AmazonS3ReadOnlyAccess policy was attached to the Developers group to allow members to view and read Amazon S3 resources, while the AmazonS3FullAccess policy was attached to the Testers group to allow members to perform S3 operations, including creating, uploading, modifying, and deleting resources. This task demonstrates how IAM groups and policies help manage AWS permissions based on users’ roles and responsibilities.
